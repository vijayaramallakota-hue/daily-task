H-tag Cheat Sheet

No Fixed Quota

There is no specific number or limit for header tags on your website.

Use of h-tags

Use h-tags to create a clear and sensible content hierarchy.

What to Avoid

Using too few can make content confusing, while too many can dilute the importance of your content.

Best Practice

Use one H1 tag per page, and multiple H2 and H3s to organize your content.

When to use H4, H5, H6

These tags are not mandatory but can be useful in certain situations.
