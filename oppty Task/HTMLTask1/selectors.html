About Team


About Sunrisers Hyderabad


Sunrisers Hyderabad is a franchise cricket team representing Hyderabad, Telangana, in the Indian Premier League (IPL).
Owned by the Sun TV Network, the team made its IPL debut in 2013 after replacing the Deccan Chargers.


Team Highlights

  • IPL Champions: 2016
  • Home Ground: Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium, Hyderabad
  • Team Colors: Orange and Black
  • Nickname: Orange Army
  • Founded: 2012
