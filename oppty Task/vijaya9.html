About srisaialam

The Srisailam Temple (Sri Bhramaramba Mallikarjuna Swamy Temple) is a major Hindu pilgrimage site in Andhra Pradesh, famous as one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and one of the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas of Goddess Parvati.

1.Location: Nallamala Hills, Nandyal district, Andhra Pradesh, on the banks of the Krishna River.
2.Deities: Lord Shiva as Mallikarjuna Swamy and Goddess Parvati as Bhramaramba Devi. It is one of the rare places housing both a Jyotirlinga and a Shakti Peetha in one complex.
3.Architecture: Built in the Dravidian style featuring tall gateways (gopurams) and detailed stone carvings.
4.Timings: Open daily from 4:30 AM to 3:30 PM and 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM (darshan hours may vary during festivals).
5.Nearby Attractions: Patalaganga (Krishna River), Sikhareswaram Temple, and Sakshi Ganapati Temple.
